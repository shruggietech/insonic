Brand essentials

Name and relationship

Approved name: insonic

Relationship: A ShruggieTech project

Written form: Write insonic in lowercase. Keep the product name distinct from file, speaker and assertion identifiers.

Approved words

Slogan
Voice, with memory.
Short description
Voice archives, speaker models, and connected knowledge.

Visual signatures

Mark: Three separated rounded voice forms establish a clear rhythm. Their staggered lengths and endpoints retain individuality; a strengthened Reduced master carries all three forms into tiny applications.

Color: Glacial blue carries the identity on midnight surfaces; deep glacial blue carries it on light surfaces. State colors remain functional, with labels and symbols accompanying them.

Type: Display Space Grotesk; body Geist; mono Geist Mono. See colors and type details.

Where each asset belongs

Use the delivered logo and mark files for their declared surfaces and sizes. The asset catalog lists each verified variant and download. The color and type sections give exact values.

The reduced mark takes over at and below 32 px.

Usage limits

Keep the delivered artwork geometry unchanged.

Implementation authority

Exact kit facts

This brand uses its own delivered interface binding; do not assume a ShruggieTech house palette. The current hosted documentation facts (JSON) declare schema version 1 and documentation contract version 1.2.0. In a downloaded kit, enforcement/documentation-facts.json governs those pinned bytes.

Interface overrides

No brand-specific interface overrides are declared. Use the delivered binding and its default rules.

Light surfaces

The bright accent #86BDF2 measures 1.84:1 on the light reading surface and is never text there. The light block substitutes #256DA9 at 5.08:1. The legal foreground on an accent fill is #000000 at 10.57:1.

Delivery

Complete asset catalog

A brand mark is the symbol alone; a logo lockup combines approved elements. Wide and stacked layouts are separate designs. Full and reduced identify source detail. Full color or monochrome identifies ink treatment. A clear background is transparent; for light or dark names the intended viewing surface. Social share images are separate opaque compositions. Preferred descriptive filenames and existing paths contain the same approved artwork. Full asset glossary.

Canvas 1000 × 1000 units, clear space 100 units (17.1 percent of artwork width). Below 32 px the reduced master takes over.

Fixed lockup proportions

lockupmark heightgapalignment
horizontal160 units34 unitsoptical center
stacked1.80C0.45Ccentered on wordmark ink width

The horizontal row records its approved master composition. C is the outlined wordmark cap height used by the stacked lockup. X is the 70-unit G channel. Keep one X clear around every master. Never resize the mark and wordmark independently.
