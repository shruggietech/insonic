insonic | Brand System02
Logo system

Marks and lockups

Three separated rounded voice forms establish a clear rhythm. Their staggered lengths and endpoints retain individuality; a strengthened Reduced master carries all three forms into tiny applications. Mark means symbol; wide and stacked lockups are separate. Clear means transparent; light and dark name the viewing surface. See Asset Language in the main manual.

Clear space

One clear-space unit on every side: 100 units on the 1000 × 1000 canvas, 17.1 percent of artwork width. No text, border, icon or crop enters that band.

Light surfaces

The bright accent #86BDF2 measures 1.84:1 on the light reading surface and is never text there. The light token block substitutes #256DA9 at 5.08:1 automatically. The legal foreground on an accent fill is #000000 at 10.57:1. Every fill token in brand.json carries its measured foreground.

Color vision

The emphasis and failure colors may not be reliably separable under deuteranopia. That is acceptable only because state is never carried by color alone: every state ships a written label.

Rules of use

Series order is fixed: chart-1 is always the primary measurement. Never reorder to make a chart look better. Never introduce a sixth color; past five series, switch to a form that does not depend on hue. A series never uses the semantic emphasis or failure colors, because those carry state.
