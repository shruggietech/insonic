# Community conduct

Contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive and specific. Critique ideas and code without harassment, threats, discrimination or publishing another person's private information. Good-faith disagreement and technical criticism are welcome.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive contributions and restrict participation proportionally to repeated or serious misconduct. Report concerns privately through the contact route on the [ShruggieTech website](https://shruggie.tech/). Maintainers handle reports discreetly and avoid exposing private details in public threads.

This policy applies to project discussions, issues, pull requests and other project-managed community spaces. It does not add content eligibility rules to the software's general-purpose processing capabilities.
