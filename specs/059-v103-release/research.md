## Historical publication and continuation

**Decision**: Preserve immutable v1.0.2 assets and exact historical validation exemptions; inspect tagged versioned docs/schema before any reconciliation. Use fresh exact-main accepted candidate bytes after owner merge.
**Rationale**: A PR merge or later rebuild cannot inherit another package's acoustic/restore acceptance. The established release workflow and public verifier already implement the required identity boundaries.
**Alternatives**: Publishing PR artifacts, broad version-based exemptions or rebuilding after acceptance would weaken authority.
