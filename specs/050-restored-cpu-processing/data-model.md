# S050 internal evidence model

- Source evidence retains its exact package/source/harness/corpus, worker environments, six processing cases, two speaker profiles and four matching cases.
- Destination evidence has separate verified worker environments and readiness before/after setup, four non-enrollment cases, fresh processing/matching work IDs, increasing recording revisions and shared model/profile identities.
- Retained authority binds canonical media/metadata and profile/model bytes plus stable completed processing/training/matching work IDs/state/results. Current-document supersession is expected; accepted retention rules do not change.
- A source-withdrawal observation requires the original path to remain absent before destination work and after final integrity checks. It never names arbitrary owner paths.
- The extended internal receipt format requires all destination stages and independently rejects missing/duplicate/reused work identities, unchanged revisions, contradictory outputs, malformed resources/reference comparisons, changed elected model/device/worker facts and failed cleanup. Historical source-only evidence retains its original meaning.
- Destination setup time remains within original qualification; all phase times are finite, nonnegative and bounded by the unchanged journey clock. Failed attempts retain their first failure and cleanup result separately.
