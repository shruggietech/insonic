# Research: Real CPU processing and speaker matching

## 2026-10-23 decisions

1. **Extend the existing connected journey.** `qualify-journey.py` already owns package election, CLI calls, durable waits, enrollment, model fetch, source withdrawal and restore. Add a reference-corpus real path using those contracts; retain controlled and historical explicit-interpreter modes. A parallel runner/framework would duplicate ownership and package verification. Read-only harness research identified missing managed preparation, independent references, playback/timeline assertions and cleanup completeness.
2. **Use OpenSLR LibriSpeech dev-clean.** The [publisher](https://www.openslr.org/12/) identifies CC BY 4.0 and independent book-derived transcripts/readers. Pin archive SHA-256 `76f87d090650617fca0cac8f88b9416e0ebf80350acb97b343a85fa903728ab3`, independently published in the [PyTorch Audio loader](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pytorch/audio/main/src/torchaudio/datasets/librispeech.py), and publisher MD5 `42e2234ba48799c1f50f24a7926300a1` from [official checksums](https://www.openslr.org/resources/12/md5sum.txt). One bounded archive acquisition is preferable to unverified downstream clip mirrors. Retain only selected original FLACs, transcript lines and attribution/metadata, then remove owned archive scratch.
3. **Freeze selection before inference.** Readers 84 and 174 are enrollment identities; 251 is unseen. Choose the first numeric 6–20-second whole utterance in the first available numeric chapter; choose held-out speech from a different chapter when available, otherwise the next distinct eligible utterance. Confirm exact IDs from the verified archive. Derive a sequence from the two held-out clips separated by exactly 8000 zero samples at 16kHz. No source clip is selected or changed based on inference.
4. **Measure only supported reference claims.** Retain substitutions/deletions/insertions and reference-word denominators under frozen normalization. Compare each match decision with independent reader/window identities, recording mixed-reference predicted voices explicitly. Clip windows include internal silence and cannot establish independently annotated speech boundaries; no DER or broad unseen-training-data guarantee is claimed. Keep frozen threshold 0.75, margin 0.1 and minimum evidence 1 s. Incorrect/unknown outcomes remain measured quality limitations, not functional passes for identification.
5. **Use normal managed and model operations.** Prepare recognition and diarization sequentially with returned `workers plan` elections and bounded durable waits. Acquire builtin choices with `models prepare`, verified local snapshots and subsequent verify/reuse previews. Existing exact snapshot metadata is available; the product must verify every hash before accepting it. Historical explicit interpreters and stale/dirty local package receipts are excluded from canonical acceptance.
6. **Retain existing inference election.** Ordinary CI runs deterministic contract/provenance/scoring/cleanup regressions and full required package/backend/native checks. Actual CPU inference remains separately elected outside CI. No ordinary CI deadline or security/integrity gate is weakened.
7. **Strengthen existing ownership proportionally.** Reuse the existing process-tree owner for hidden children and descendant cleanup; isolate TEMP/TMP alongside profile variables, make receipt replacement atomic, validate required stages/cases/identities before success, and preserve first failures through cleanup. No owner libraries, credentials or installed state are modified.

Historical S015/S025 measurements establish earlier real execution; they are not current-package evidence. S015 matching returned unknown at 0.677446 against 0.75, and dialogue diarization counts did not establish independent accuracy. Read-only corpus and harness research were dispatched under the installed Spec Kit plan instruction; no research process, inference, file mutation or public comment was delegated.
